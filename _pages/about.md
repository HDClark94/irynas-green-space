Iryna's Green Space is one room doing four things that belong together: a
**refill shop** so you can buy what you need without buying packaging again, an
**eco laundry** that washes gently and cleanly, a **sound therapy** room, and a
**safe space** that is open whether or not you are here to buy anything.

The four fit together because they come from the same idea — that the ordinary
business of looking after yourself and your home shouldn't cost the place you
live in, and shouldn't be something you do alone.

## What we stand for

**Less packaging, not more guilt.** Refilling should be the easy option, not the
effortful one. Bring a container, or take one of ours and bring it back.

**Gentle on fabric, gentle on water.** What goes down the drain ends up
somewhere. The laundry runs on low temperatures, full loads and detergents that
break down.

**Rest is not a luxury.** The sound room is there because looking after
yourself shouldn't require a reason, an appointment, or a diagnosis.

**Open to everyone.** Somewhere to sit, warm up, charge a phone or wait for
someone. No purchase necessary, and nobody asked why they are here.

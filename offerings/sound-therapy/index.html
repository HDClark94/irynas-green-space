Sound therapy

A quiet room, a sound bath, and an hour that asks nothing of you.

A quiet room with instruments, where you lie down and listen. Nothing is required of you — no experience, no particular belief about what it does, and no need to say anything.

What happens

You lie down, usually on a mat with a blanket. The session is mostly listening. People often fall asleep, which is fine.

Is it for me?

Buy as much or as little as you need, in a container you already own. Bring
jars, bottles, tubs or bags — we weigh them empty first, so you only pay for
what goes in. If you forget, we keep clean containers you can take and return.

## How it works

1. Bring your containers, or borrow ours
2. We weigh them empty
3. Fill what you need
4. Weigh again and pay the difference

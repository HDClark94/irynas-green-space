## Your right to cancel

If you are buying as a consumer in the EU or UK, you may cancel within **14 days**
of receiving the goods, for any reason, and receive a refund.

To cancel, tell us within those 14 days. Return the goods within 14 days of
telling us, and we will refund within 14 days of getting them back.

## Faulty or damaged goods

Instruments are inspected before despatch. If something arrives damaged, tell us
within a reasonable period and we will replace or refund it. This is in addition
to the right to cancel above, not instead of it.

Hand-hammered instruments vary in finish, tone and small marks. That is a feature
of how they are made rather than a fault.

Singing bowls & instruments

Hand-hammered bowls, tingsha and other instruments from Nepal — come and play them before you buy.

A small collection of sound healing instruments, most of them made in Nepal. Bowls are hand-hammered rather than cast, so no two sound alike — which is why everything here is out on the shelf to be played rather than boxed up.

Come and listen. Choosing a bowl is mostly a matter of finding the one whose note you want to keep hearing.

What we carry

  • Singing bowls — hand-hammered, various sizes and notes
  • Tingsha — paired cymbals, for opening and closing a session
  • Mallets and strikers — suede, felt and wood, each giving a different voice
  • Cushions and rings — so a bowl can sit and ring freely
  • Bells and dorje
  • Incense and holders

Trying before buying

Everything is playable. Ask, and we will take it down and show you how to strike and rim it — a bowl that sounds thin in the wrong hands is usually just being held wrong.

If you are buying for a practice rather than a room, bring an instrument you already own and we will help you find one that sits with it.

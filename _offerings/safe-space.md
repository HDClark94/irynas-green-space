Part of the shop is just a place to be. Chairs, warmth, water, a plug socket and
a toilet. No purchase necessary, and nobody will ask you why you are here.

## What that means in practice

- Sit as long as you like during opening hours
- Free drinking water, and somewhere to charge a phone
- A toilet you can use without asking
- Nobody is moved on for not buying anything
